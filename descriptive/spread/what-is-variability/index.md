<section class="section">
  <div class="section-head">
    <h1>What Is Variability?</h1>
    <p class="lead">
      Measures of center describe what is typical.  
      Measures of spread describe how different the observations are from each other.
    </p>
  </div>

  <hr>

  <h2>1. Why Spread Matters</h2>

  <p>
    Two datasets can have the same mean but behave very differently.
  </p>

  <p><strong>Example:</strong></p>

  <p>Dataset A: 48, 49, 50, 51, 52</p>
  <p>Dataset B: 10, 30, 50, 70, 90</p>

  <p>
    Both datasets have mean = 50.
  </p>

  <p>
    But Dataset A is tightly clustered around 50,  
    while Dataset B is widely dispersed.
  </p>

  <p>
    The mean alone does not tell the full story.
  </p>

  <hr>

  <h2>2. Definition</h2>

  <p>
    <strong>Variability</strong> (or dispersion) describes how far observations spread around the center.
  </p>

  <p>
    It answers:
    <strong>“How consistent or inconsistent are the values?”</strong>
  </p>

  <hr>

  <h2>3. Conceptual View</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Low variability → values are close together</li>
    <li>High variability → values are widely spread</li>
    <li>No variability → all values are identical</li>
  </ul>

  <hr>

  <h2>4. Why We Need Measures of Spread</h2>

  <p>Spread helps us:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Assess stability (exam scores, production quality)</li>
    <li>Evaluate risk (financial returns)</li>
    <li>Compare consistency between groups</li>
    <li>Understand reliability of averages</li>
  </ul>

  <hr>

  <h2>5. Relationship Between Center and Spread</h2>

  <p>
    Describing a dataset properly requires both:
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>Measure of center (mean or median)</li>
    <li>Measure of spread (range, IQR, SD, etc.)</li>
  </ul>

  <p>
    Reporting only one of them is incomplete.
  </p>

  <hr>

  <h2>Summary</h2>

  <ul>
    <li>Variability describes how dispersed values are.</li>
    <li>Two datasets can share the same mean but differ in spread.</li>
    <li>Measures of spread complete the descriptive picture.</li>
  </ul>
