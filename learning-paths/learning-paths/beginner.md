# Beginner Learning Path

Start from zero and build a strong, correct understanding of statistics.  
Follow the steps in order — each topic builds on the previous one.

---

## Step 1: Foundations

Understand the basics:
- What is data
- Types of variables
- Measurement scales
- Statistical thinking

👉 [Start Foundations →](/foundations/)

---

## Step 2: Descriptive Statistics

Learn how to summarize and explore data:
- Mean, median, mode
- Variance and standard deviation
- Data visualization

👉 [Go to Descriptive →](/descriptive/)

---

## Step 3: Probability

Understand randomness and distributions:
- Probability rules
- Random variables
- Distributions

👉 [Go to Probability →](/probability/)

---

## Step 4: Statistical Inference

Make conclusions from data:
- Confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing
- p-values

👉 [Go to Inference →](/inference/)

---

## Step 5: Introduction to Modeling

Start understanding relationships:
- Regression basics
- Interpretation of results

👉 [Go to Modeling →](/modeling/)

---

## Practice (Recommended)

Use software alongside each step:
- SPSS
- R
- Python
- Excel

👉 [Practice with Software →](/software/)

---

## Important Notes

- Do not skip probability before inference  
- Focus on understanding, not memorization  
- Practice with real data whenever possible
