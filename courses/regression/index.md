<!-- ABOUT -->
<section class="section" id="about-the-course">
  <div class="section-head">
    <h2>About the course</h2>
    <p>
      This course develops regression analysis from first principles to modern methods. Every result
      is derived, every example can be reproduced in R and Python, and every method is interpreted in
      context. It runs from simple linear regression through the matrix theory of least squares,
      diagnostics, and model selection, to shrinkage estimation under multicollinearity.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid-3">

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Who it is for</h3>
      <p>Upper-level undergraduates, graduate students, and researchers who use regression and want to understand why it works, not just how to run it.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Prerequisites</h3>
      <ul class="bullets">
        <li>Introductory probability and statistical inference</li>
        <li>Basic calculus</li>
        <li>Linear algebra (matrices, inverse, rank), needed from Module 2 on</li>
        <li>Some R or Python is helpful but not required</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Each lesson includes</h3>
      <ul class="bullets">
        <li>Learning objectives</li>
        <li>Theory and derivations</li>
        <li>A worked example</li>
        <li>R and Python code</li>
        <li>Exercises with solutions</li>
        <li>Interactive demos where they help understanding</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>

<!-- COURSE MAP -->
<section class="section section-soft" id="course-map">
  <div class="section-head">
    <h2>Course map</h2>
    <p>Five modules, each building on the previous one. Lessons are published module by module.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid-3">

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Module 1: Foundations</h3>
      <p>Simple linear regression, ordinary least squares, inference, and confidence and prediction intervals.</p>
      <p class="muted-mini">Status: In preparation</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Module 2: Multiple Regression</h3>
      <p>The matrix formulation, properties of the OLS estimator, hypothesis tests, ANOVA, and categorical predictors.</p>
      <p class="muted-mini">Status: In preparation</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Module 3: Diagnostics</h3>
      <p>Assumptions, residual analysis, heteroscedasticity, autocorrelation, multicollinearity, and influence.</p>
      <p class="muted-mini">Status: In preparation</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Module 4: Model Selection and Validation</h3>
      <p>Information criteria, cross-validation, and the bias–variance tradeoff.</p>
      <p class="muted-mini">Status: In preparation</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Module 5: Advanced Methods</h3>
      <p>Ridge, Liu, and Liu-type estimators, lasso, elastic net, and principal component regression.</p>
      <p class="muted-mini">Status: In preparation</p>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>

<!-- OUTCOMES -->
<section class="section" id="learning-outcomes">
  <div class="section-head">
    <h2>Learning outcomes</h2>
    <p>By the end of the course, students can:</p>
  </div>
  <ul class="bullets">
    <li>derive the OLS estimators and their properties;</li>
    <li>carry out and interpret inference in regression;</li>
    <li>diagnose and remedy violations of the model assumptions;</li>
    <li>select and validate models for prediction;</li>
    <li>apply and compare shrinkage estimators under multicollinearity.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
