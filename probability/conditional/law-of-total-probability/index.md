<h2>Formula</h2>
<p>If {B₁,...,Bₙ} partitions the sample space:</p>
<p><strong>P(A) = Σ P(A | Bᵢ) P(Bᵢ)</strong></p>
</section>

<section class="section">
<h2>Why It Matters</h2>

<li>Break complex problems into cases</li>
