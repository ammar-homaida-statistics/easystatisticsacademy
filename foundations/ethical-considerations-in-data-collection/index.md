<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Why this topic matters</h2>

    <p>
      Data are collected from people, organizations,
      and real-world processes.
    </p>

    <p>
      Poor ethical choices can cause real harm,
      even when data analysis is technically correct.
    </p>

    <p>
      Ethical failures undermine trust in statistics
      and in the institutions that use data.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Consent</h2>

    <p>
      Informed consent means that participants
      understand what data are being collected
      and how they will be used.
    </p>

    <p>
      Consent should be voluntary and based
      on clear, honest information.
    </p>

    <p>
      Collecting data without consent raises
      serious ethical concerns.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Privacy</h2>

    <p>
      Privacy concerns how personal information
      is protected and handled.
    </p>

    <p>
      Even anonymized data can sometimes
      be linked back to individuals.
    </p>

    <p>
      Ethical data collection minimizes
      unnecessary exposure of personal details.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Data misuse</h2>

    <p>
      Data misuse occurs when data are used
      in ways that participants did not agree to.
    </p>

    <p>
      This includes misleading analysis,
      unauthorized sharing, or harmful applications.
    </p>

    <p>
      Ethical responsibility extends beyond
      data collection to data use.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Why ethics is a statistical issue</h2>

    <p>
      Statistical decisions influence how data
      are collected, analyzed, and interpreted.
    </p>

    <p>
      Ethical considerations shape study design,
      not just data handling.
    </p>

    <p>
      Responsible statistics requires both
      technical skill and ethical judgment.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section section-soft">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Key idea to remember</h2>

    <p>
      Ethical data collection is essential.
      Statistical correctness alone is not enough.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

    <p>
      Ethics guide how data are collected and used.
      Consent, privacy, and responsible use
