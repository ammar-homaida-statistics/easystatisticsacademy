<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Why this topic matters</h2>

    <p>
      Most real-world decisions involve incomplete information.
    </p>

    <p>
      We typically observe samples,
      not entire populations.
    </p>

    <p>
      Inferential statistics provides a structured way
      to reason beyond observed data.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Why inference is needed</h2>

    <p>
      It is rarely possible to measure
      every member of a population.
    </p>

    <p>
      Instead, we collect data from a subset
      and seek to generalize.
    </p>

    <p>
      Inference formalizes this process.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>From sample to population</h2>

    <p>
      A sample provides observable statistics.
    </p>

    <p>
      The population contains unknown parameters.
    </p>

    <p>
      Inferential methods connect statistics
      to parameters through structured reasoning.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Uncertainty is unavoidable</h2>

    <p>
      Because samples vary,
      conclusions cannot be perfectly certain.
    </p>

    <p>
      Different samples would produce
      different statistics.
    </p>

    <p>
      Uncertainty reflects this variability.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Uncertainty as a feature, not a flaw</h2>

    <p>
      Uncertainty does not weaken statistics.
    </p>

    <p>
      It makes assumptions explicit
      and quantifies limits of knowledge.
    </p>

    <p>
      Responsible inference acknowledges
      and measures uncertainty.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section section-soft">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Key idea to remember</h2>

    <p>
      Inferential statistics connects samples to populations
      by reasoning under uncertainty.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section section-next">
  <div class="content-narrow">
    <h2>Simple summary</h2>

    <p>
      Inferential statistics allows us to generalize
      from sample data to population conclusions,
      while explicitly accounting for uncertainty.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
